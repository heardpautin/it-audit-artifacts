# Evidence & Sampling Approach

## Population Identification
Populations may include:
- User access listings
- Role assignments
- Change management logs
- System-generated reports or queries

Population completeness and accuracy are validated before sampling.

---

## Sampling Method
Sampling is risk-based and objective:
- Sample size determined by control criticality
- Selection avoids bias
- Rationale is documented

---

## Evidence Evaluation
Evidence is reviewed to confirm:
- Proper approval
- Appropriate access
- Timely execution
- Alignment with policy

---

## Exception Identification
An exception is noted when evidence does not meet criteria.

Each exception includes:
- Condition
- Criteria
- Cause
- Impact

Exceptions are validated with control owners for accuracy only.
