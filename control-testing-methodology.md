# Control Testing Methodology

## Purpose
Control testing is performed to determine whether IT controls are designed appropriately and operating effectively during the audit period.

---

## Control Understanding
For each control, the auditor documents:
- Control objective
- Control owner
- Expected operation
- Associated risk

---

## Testing Approach
Testing includes:
- Reviewing approvals and authorization
- Validating timeliness and completeness
- Verifying alignment to policy requirements
- Comparing evidence to control criteria

---

## Independence Boundary
Auditors do not:
- Modify access
- Approve changes
- Implement remediation

Auditors do:
- Assess evidence
- Identify deviations
- Document exceptions
- Escalate results

---

## Conclusion
Each control is concluded as:
- Effective
- Ineffective
- Effective with exceptions
