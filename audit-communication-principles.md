# Audit Communication Principles

## Approach
Audit communication is professional, factual, and policy-based.

The auditor:
- Anchors discussions to control requirements
- Avoids emotional or subjective language
- Focuses on evidence, not intent

---

## Handling Pushback
When resistance occurs:
- The auditor restates criteria
- Documents management perspective
- Maintains independence

---

## Goal
The goal is not confrontation, but clarity:
- Clear findings
- Accurate documentation
- No surprises in reporting
