# IT Audit & Control Testing – Practical Artifacts

## Overview
This repository demonstrates a practical, real-world approach to IT audit and control testing in regulated environments. The focus is on independently assessing the design and operating effectiveness of IT controls through evidence evaluation, sampling, and documentation.

The artifacts reflect how IT auditors evaluate controls such as logical access, segregation of duties, and change management without owning or modifying the controls themselves.

---

## Audit Lifecycle Overview
The audit process follows a repeatable lifecycle:

1. Planning & Scoping
2. Control Understanding
3. Population Identification
4. Sampling
5. Testing & Evidence Evaluation
6. Exception Identification
7. Management Communication
8. Reporting & Conclusion
9. Repeat Cycle

This lifecycle is applied consistently across control domains.

---

## Control Domains Covered
- Logical Access Controls
- Segregation of Duties (SoD)
- Change Management Controls

---

## Auditor Independence
This project emphasizes auditor independence:
- Controls are **evaluated**, not designed or remediated
- Exceptions are **identified and documented**, not fixed
- Management owns remediation decisions
- Audit provides objective assessment and reporting
